# ADR-0015 — Direct consumer subscription; free is mechanical, paid is the half that knows you

- **Date:** 2026-08-28
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Needed a pricing model that survives its own unit economics (M9) rather than one chosen on vibes.
- **Decision:** Free tier: blocking, review, ledger (no inference cost). Paid tier: plan, judge, companion's judgment, memory, coach — the half of the product that costs inference. The paywall sits exactly where the inference cost sits.
- **Consequences:** Pricing and cost structure stay coupled by construction — a cost increase in the paid half is a pricing conversation, not a surprise.
- **Source:** `docs/prd-intent.md` §7, Q6
