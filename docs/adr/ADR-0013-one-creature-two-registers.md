# ADR-0013 — One creature, two registers: witness during the session, coach in the review

- **Date:** 2026-08-28
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Splitting "the thing that watches you" and "the thing that talks to you" into two separate entities would cost the continuity that makes memory feel like a relationship, and the authority that makes advice land.
- **Decision:** A single entity (the companion) plays both roles — silent witness during a session, speaking coach only in the review. I3 resolves the apparent contradiction: the coach answers to the evidence, never to the outcome answer.
- **Consequences:** The companion's visual form later reversed (ADR-0026), but this structural decision — one creature, register changes with the moment — held through that reversal unchanged.
