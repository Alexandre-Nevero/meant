# ADR-0010 — Primary user moves from employed corporate administrator to self-employed browser worker

- **Date:** 2026-08-28
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** The old persona (employed, managed corporate endpoint) had no purchasing authority — the only party with budget was the employer, whom `docs/idea-intent.md` §9 forbids serving, permanently. The product had designed itself into having no legal buyer.
- **Decision:** Primary user redefined as self-employed, non-technical, browser-native — owns her own laptop and her own credit card.
- **Consequences:** Unblocks a real business model (ADR-0015). Ripples into `PRODUCT.md` §1, `docs/prd-intent.md` §2, and rules out any manager/team view, permanently.
- **Source:** `docs/idea-intent.md` §1, `docs/prd-intent.md` §2
